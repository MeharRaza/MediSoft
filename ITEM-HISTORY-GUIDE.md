# Item History Feature - User Guide

## 📍 Location: Sales History Page

---

## 🎯 Quick Access

**Path:** Sales → Sales History → Item History Button

---

## 📖 Step-by-Step Instructions

### Step 1: Open Sales History
```
Sidebar → Sales → Sales History
```

### Step 2: Click "Item History" Button
- Located in top-right corner
- Next to the "Refresh" button
- Has a history icon

### Step 3: Search for Product
- Type product name (e.g., "Panadol")
- OR type generic name (e.g., "Paracetamol")
- Results appear automatically
- Shows up to 20 matching products

### Step 4: View History
- Click "View History" button next to any product
- Wait for data to load (1-2 seconds)

### Step 5: Review Sales Data

**Summary Statistics (Top Row):**
- Total Sales Count
- Total Quantity Sold
- Average Sale Price
- Maximum Sale Price
- Minimum Sale Price

**Sales History Table (Last 50 Sales):**
- Invoice Number
- Sale Date & Time
- Customer Name
- Quantity Sold
- Sale Price
- Total Amount
- Cashier Name

### Step 6: Navigate
- Click "← Back to search" to search another product
- Click "Close" button to exit
- OR click outside modal to close

### 1. Customer Inquiry
**Scenario:** Customer asks "What did I pay for this medicine last time?"

**Steps:**
1. Go to Sales History → Item History

1. Open Item History

**Scenario:** Need to trace who sold what and when

## 🔍 Search Tips

### What You Can Search:
- ✅ Product name (exact or partial)
- ✅ Generic name (exact or partial)
- ✅ Mixed case (search is case-insensitive)

### Examples:
- Search "pana" → finds "Panadol", "Panadol Extra", etc.
- Search "para" → finds all products with "Paracetamol"
- Search "BRUF" → finds "Brufen" (case doesn't matter)

## 📊 What's Included in Stats

### Total Sales
- Count of all sales transactions
- Excludes voided sales

### Total Quantity
- Sum of all units sold
- Across all sales

### Average Price
- Mean sale price
- Calculated from all sales

### Max Price
- Highest sale price ever recorded
- Useful for price checking

### Min Price
- Lowest sale price ever recorded
- Useful for discount verification

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Why only last 50 sales?**
A: For performance. 50 sales is enough for most use cases. If you need more, contact support.

**Q: Can I export this data?**
A: Currently no. Feature may be added in future.

**Q: What if product has no sales?**
A: Modal shows "No sales history found for this product"

**Q: Does it show voided sales?**
A: No, voided sales are excluded from history.

**Q: Can I filter by date range?**
A: Not yet. Shows all-time history (last 50 sales).

**Q: Can I see which batch was sold?**
A: Not in this view. Check individual invoice details for batch info.
